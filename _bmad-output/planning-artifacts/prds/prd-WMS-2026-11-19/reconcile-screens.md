# Reconcile: screen mockups vs PRD

Severity scale:
- **High**: a Phase 1 capability, control or legal obligation is missing or contradicted, and stories would be wrong without it.
- **Medium**: a real rule or capability is missing; the build team would have to invent it.
- **Low**: a detail rule worth adding to an FR or the catalogue; no story is blocked.
