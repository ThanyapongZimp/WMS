# Digest: customer-voice r1

Scope: what JST ERP (Thailand) users say about it. Round 1 used web search and web fetch only, accessed 2026-11-19. Budget used: 17 tool calls; 7 distinct sources read in full (App Store TH, BigSeller blog, ASAP Project blog, PEAK manual, Sina Heimao complaint, Google Play TH search, Zhihu [blocked 403]), plus search-result snippets.

**Headline finding: there is very little first-hand Thai user voice that search engines can find.** No Pantip thread, public Facebook group post, Blockdit post or Reddit post about JST ERP turned up. The only first-hand Thai user voice found is 4 App Store reviews of the "Jster" app. All other Thai material is vendor PR, competitor content or partner/affiliate content. Weigh every row below with this in mind.

## Contradictions

- **WMS strength vs. "basic WMS".** BigSeller (a competitor) frames JST as the WMS-heavy choice. The ASAP partner review calls JST's WMS "ขั้นต้น" (basic) and says the full WMS is gated to the top tier. Both may hold: JST is strong compared with simple multichannel tools, but its WMS is entry-level compared with a dedicated WMS. **This is a possible wedge, but it rests on non-user sources.**
- **Scale vs. user footprint.** JST claims large scale (vendor figures, not verified here), yet its Thai app has 32 App Store ratings. Most users probably work in the web console, so app ratings likely under-represent the user base.
- **Ease of use.** One App Store reviewer says the app is enjoyable to use ("Jihny25"), while another says it is very hard to use.
