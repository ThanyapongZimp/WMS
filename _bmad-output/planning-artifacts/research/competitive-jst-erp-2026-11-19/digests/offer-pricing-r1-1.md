## Contradictions between sources
- **China market share:** the JST Thai HKEX post and the prospectus-derived snippet say 24.4% (by 2024 e-commerce SaaS ERP revenue). A broker IPO note quoted in search snippets says 8.7% (by total SaaS revenue). These use different denominators.
- **Revenue:** the Thai news post says about 1,098.1M yuan in 2024. A TMTPost summary (via search snippet) says 9.10亿 (910M) in 2024. The likely explanation is a different metric (total vs. SaaS revenue, or a different period). This needs the prospectus to resolve.
- **API openness:** the homepage claims an "Open API", but the ToS bans automated access and requires manual use (claims 14 vs 15).
- **Founding date:** WorldFirst gives "founded 2021" for the Thai entity, while Jushuitan itself dates from 2014 (TMTPost). These are consistent if 2021 refers to the Thai subsidiary, but that is unconfirmed.
