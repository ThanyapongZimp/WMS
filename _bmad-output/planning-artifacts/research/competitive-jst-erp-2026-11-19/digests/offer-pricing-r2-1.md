## Contradictions
- **Customer counts:** 88,400 (2024 full year) vs 66,100 (H1 2024) vs 92,600 (30 Jun 2025). These may be "served during period" vs "point-in-time" counts. The exact definitions are unverified.
- **Pricing model:** the same article calls JST "typical subscription SaaS" and also credits growth to "per-order-volume billing". Most likely this is a subscription metered by order volume, but that is unverified.
- **IPO size:** HK$2.086bn gross planned (pedaily) vs HK$1.94bn net (Tiger), plus about HK$300M greenshoe. These differ in gross vs net basis and are consistent.
- **Thai headcount:** 11-50 (JobsDB) vs 51-200 (WorldFirst, r1).
- **Origin story:** WorldFirst says JST was "initially focused on warehouse management", and TMTPost says it was centred on back-end warehouse management. This is consistent and supports WMS being core to JST, not peripheral.
