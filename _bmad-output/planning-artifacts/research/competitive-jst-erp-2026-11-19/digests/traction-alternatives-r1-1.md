## Leads worth chasing

1. **JST Group 2025 annual report / 2026 interim report (HKEX 6687).** These are primary filings and the only route to an overseas or SEA revenue and customer breakout. Read the "overseas" section via HKEX "disclosure of interests / segment" notes, or fetch smaller split PDFs.
2. **JST Thailand integration and partner pages.** Check jsterp.com / jst-erp Thai site customer logos, Facebook/LINE case posts, and marketplace partner lists, including Shopee/Lazada/TikTok certified ERP lists. Third-party 3PL "integrates with JST" pages would let us count JST-running warehouses directly. This is the best proxy for the installed base.
3. **Jobsdb / LinkedIn postings for "JST ERP" Bangkok.** Read via another fetch route. Headcount (11-50 vs 51-200) and roles (implementation, warehouse consultant, sales) show the scale and whether it is pushing into the WMS/3PL segment.
4. **Bangkok Post "E-commerce to fuel rise of express deliveries" and the Thailand Post / Flash / J&T volume disclosures.** These give independent corroboration of the 7-8M parcels/day figure.
5. **Flash Fulfillment, Shippop/Anywhere-type 3PLs, and Lazada/Shopee fulfillment.** Sellers who outsource to a 3PL with its own WMS are outside JST's warehouse footprint. That shrinks the addressable "JST warehouses" pool.

## Contradictions

- **JST Thailand headcount.** Jobsdb says 11-50 (#4); WorldFirst says 51-200 (#2).
- **JST founding date.** WorldFirst gives "Thailand, 2021" (Thai entity), while the Jobsdb snippet says "ก่อตั้งขึ้นในปี 2014" (founded 2014, the parent's founding year). These are probably the entity versus the group, not a true conflict.
- **Thai CEP market size.** VNA/ShipHub gives THB 115bn (about US$3.4bn, 2024); Mordor gives about US$2.86bn (2025). The difference likely comes from definitions; neither is reconciled.
- **Scale of the comparison set.** Vendor claims of BigSeller "110,000+ Thai sellers" and Page365 "350,000+ shops" dwarf JST's "<1k clients". These count shops/sellers, not warehouses, so they are not like-for-like. Even so, on available evidence JST is not the dominant system by seller count in Thailand. Its niche appears to be warehouse-heavy sellers.
